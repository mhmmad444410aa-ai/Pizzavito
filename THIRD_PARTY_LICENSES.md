# Third-party assets
The fetch script selects food and kitchen models only.
- Kenney Food Kit 2.0 — https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit — CC0 1.0.
- KayKit Restaurant Bits — https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Restaurant-Bits-1.0 — CC0 1.0, pinned upstream revision 153c8a7535b48237854cb54ff6890679f8c574d1.
Actual imported files and hashes are written to assets/imported/manifest.json and uploaded by CI. No external music. Other proposed packs are not included until their redistribution terms and content are verified.
